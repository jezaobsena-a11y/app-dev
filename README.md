+ # Title: Jujutsu Kaisen

+ ---

+ ## overview
+ Jujutsu Kaisen is a story about Yuji Itadori, a high school student who becomes the unwilling host to a powerful curse, Ryomen Sukuna, after he swallows one of Sukuna's fingers. To find a way to be exorcised without dying, Yuji joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers and enrolls in a technical college to learn how to control cursed energy and fight other curses. His journey involves battling supernatural creatures, grappling with the immense power of Sukuna inside him, and learning the moral complexities of a world filled with curses, sorcerers, and the fight between good and evil.


+ ---

+ ## why is jujutsu kaisen is iconic
+ – Jujutsu Sorcerers: Individuals with the ability to manipulate "cursed energy" to fight curses.
+ – Cursed Energy: A mysterious energy that comes from negative emotions in humans and can create cursed spirits.
+ – Technical College: Yuji enrolls in a school for sorcerers to learn how to control his power and eventually be able to consume all of Sukuna's fingers so that he can be fully exorcised.
